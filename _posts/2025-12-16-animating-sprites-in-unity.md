I created a new animation folder in Unity projects folder and created a new animation for PowerUp for Triple Shot.

![Picture from this post](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*CpfXRrbVlBeGpSfnVvamXQ.gif)

And then I chose the Object and go to Animation window, after that by pressing record button I’m starting to record animation, and in animation timeline I select all Sprites which was made kind of frames for each second or milliseconds and drop them all, which will give me the animation of glowing PowerUp, and like this I get a beautiful glowing PowerUp

![Picture from this post](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*WCqh12CfcCA7xWtM2sW7qA.gif)
