**Chapter-1(How to easily navigate in Unity)**

To easily navigate and fly through your scene you need to press the right button of your mouse and use the “**W A S D**” buttons in your keyboard as in video games and you will be able to control you environment easily.

**Chapter-2(How to create a material in Unity)**

For this you must go to Assets folder and right click with mouse and go to create button and choose create folder and rename it as Materials.

![Picture from this post](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*RhIxYdL9DdhAF0t63zWh7w.gif)

And then go to Materials folder and right press again and choose Create → Material and rename it and choose the color with color picker on the right side.

![Picture from this post](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*xlO9i1q5mMS3J0DY0jrdeA.gif)

After that to set a material for given object jus gram the material with left click of mouse to object and everything is done

![Picture from this post](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*p_Wb88jFhRk5LACcLyBezQ.gif)

**Chapter-3(The scenes or levels)**

The scenes or Levels in the game is very interesting thing.

To create a new scene or Level, you must go to left top corner and clickk to File →New scene →Create and like this you created the new scene or the new level.

![Picture from this post](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*rABGYzgygsVggymfqR_Ycg.gif)

And here you can build and create a new objects.
